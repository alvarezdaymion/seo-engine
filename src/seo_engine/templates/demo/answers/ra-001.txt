Good question. That popping or rumbling sound usually means sediment has built up at the base of the tank. Minerals in the water settle out over time and form a crusty layer. When the burner heats the tank, water trapped under that layer boils and pushes up through it, and that is the pop you hear.

A thick layer of sediment can make the burner run about 10 percent longer to heat the same water. Um, so the first thing I would do is flush the tank. You turn off the power or the gas, hook a garden hose to the drain valve, and let it run until the water comes out clear.

Most homes should do that once a year, more often if you have hard water. Honestly, if you flush it every year, that tank is guaranteed to last twenty years. If the popping keeps going after a flush, or you see water around the base, give us a call and we will take a look.
